✨ Main Features of Sky Vision:

Ai Agent:

💬 Smart Assistant
Your personal AI assistant that is always at hand. Make calls, write emails and SMS, set timers, alarms, and reminders, create and pin notes, ask questions, and get answers.
- Instant summaries: select a long text or article, or open the screen you need, and the Assistant will create a brief summary of the key points.

✍️ WriteHub — Convenience in Text Processing
Your ultimate productivity toolkit!
- Text Improvement: fix mistakes, rephrase sentences, and change the tone (business, friendly, etc.) in one click.
- AI Chat in the input field: write your request right in the WhatsApp or Telegram chat bar (e.g., "write a polite refusal"), press the button, and WriteHub replaces it with a detailed, ready-made answer or searches the internet.
- WriteHub Summaries: get a brief overview of text from input fields or summarize web pages on the fly.
- Quick commands for text correction, translation, or queries. For example, use web search in a command to fact-check an article with a short trigger like !fc. Hide commands in the QC folder to keep your overlay tidy.
- My Space quick access: insert addresses, phone numbers, and emails from your contacts and notes into your text without switching apps.

🔔 AlertBrief — Smart Notification Summaries
Tired of an endless stream of messages? AlertBrief collects incoming notifications and uses AI to turn them into short, clear summaries.

⚡ PriorityFlow — AI Importance Filter
Set up semantic AI rules for push notifications. PriorityFlow analyzes incoming messages and lets only the most important ones through. It can even bypass Do Not Disturb for urgent emails or VIP notifications. No more spam! Standard non-AI rules are also available.

📧 Email Agent
Your personal email secretary.
- Intelligent email reading and brief summaries of long threads.
- Automatic smart, contextual draft replies.
- Write to multiple recipients at once.
- Thanks to My Space, saved tags, and sender names, you can simply say: "Write to Sarah's work email that the meeting is moved to Monday."

🤖 AI Tasks & Tasks
Automate your life!
- Tasks: a convenient manager for daily tasks and reminders. Ask the Assistant or use Chat to remind you about something or attach a note.
- AI Tasks: background automations that the AI executes for you on a schedule.

🗨️ AI Chat
A dedicated chat screen for deep, long conversations with the neural network. Generate ideas, discuss projects, and solve complex problems with context from your long-term AI memory (Smart Memory). Code generation with file saving is available right in the chat.

🧠 My Space — Unified Intellectual Memory
My Space connects Assistant, WriteHub, and Chat into a single ecosystem. It is your personal context space: the AI knows your preferences, important contacts, notes, and project details, so it understands you without long explanations. In contacts you can add names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, notes, and email styles. Fill in your own details and the AI can instantly complete forms, emails, and more.

🔒 Privacy
Your keys, chat history, rules, My Space data, and notes are stored strictly locally on your device. The local Gemini Nano model is also available for some features.

♿ Accessibility API Permission
Sky Vision uses the Accessibility Service API solely for WriteHub: to detect text being typed in input fields and to replace it with the result (corrected text, AI reply, or command output). The permission is not used for any other purpose.

Important Notes:
- Free providers and models do not guarantee uninterrupted access.
- Gemini Nano is not available on all devices.
