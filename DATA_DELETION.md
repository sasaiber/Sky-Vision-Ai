# Data Deletion Instructions for Sky Vision

In compliance with Google Play's Data Safety policies, this page provides information on how to manage and delete your data associated with the **Sky Vision** application.

## 1. Account Deletion (Google OAuth)
Sky Vision allows account creation using Google OAuth to provide access to free AI models and web search features. If you wish to delete your account and any associated data from our servers, you can submit a deletion request.

**How to request account deletion:**
1. Send an email with your deletion request to **roslovtsev167@gmail.com** (or contact us via our official Telegram community: [https://t.me/i_sky_vision_i](https://t.me/i_sky_vision_i)).
2. Please send the request from the email address associated with your Google OAuth account, or specify it in your message for verification.
3. State clearly that you wish to delete your Sky Vision account and all associated server data.

We will process your request and delete your account data within 30 days.

*(Note: Our server is used exclusively for providing access to free AI models and web search. We do not store your personal chat history or screen contents on our servers).*

## 2. How to Delete Your Local Data
Most of your data—including your chat history, custom AI rules, API keys, and app preferences—is stored **locally** on your device's private storage, not on our servers.

You have full control over this local data and can delete it entirely at any time by following these steps:

**Option A: Clear App Data**
1. Open your Android device's **Settings**.
2. Go to **Apps** or **App Management**.
3. Find and select **Sky Vision**.
4. Tap on **Storage & cache**.
5. Tap **Clear storage** or **Clear data**. 
*(This will reset the app and delete all locally saved preferences).*

**Option B: Uninstall the App**
1. Long-press the Sky Vision app icon on your home screen or app drawer.
2. Select **Uninstall**.
*(This will completely remove the app and all of its locally stored data).*

## 3. Third-Party AI Providers
If you use third-party cloud AI providers (via your own API keys) within the app, any data transmitted to them is governed by their respective privacy policies. To manage or delete data held by those third parties, please refer to their specific data management policies.
