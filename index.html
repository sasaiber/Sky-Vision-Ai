Why is AI Task distributed as a separate app?

Policy Compliance & Maximum User Freedom

⚖️ Google Play Policy Clarification

Google Play updated its Developer Program Policies regarding the Accessibility API. Store-distributed apps are prohibited from using accessibility services to autonomously plan, initiate, or execute background actions and automated device decisions.

🚀 Zero Compromises for Users

Rather than crippling advanced automation capabilities in Sky Vision, we isolated the autonomous engine into this free, open-source companion module. The main Sky Vision app remains 100% compliant with Google Play.

🔒 100% Transparent & Private

The companion module is fully open source on GitHub. It runs locally on your device, communicates exclusively with your selected AI models, and contains zero trackers, analytics, or hidden background services.

⚡ Instant Seamless Integration

Once installed, Sky Vision automatically detects the AI Task Module via secure IPC. The AI Task card on your Home screen unlocks instantly with no extra setup required.
