Why is AI Task distributed as a separate app?

Policy Compliance & Direct User Control

Policy Compliance

Google Play Policy Update

Google Play updated its Developer Program Guidelines regarding the Accessibility API. Store-distributed apps are prohibited from performing autonomous background device execution and automated routines.

Full Control

Zero Capabilities Lost

To keep the primary Sky Vision application 100% compliant with Google Play while preserving powerful automated routines, background actions are isolated into this dedicated companion app.

Privacy & Ownership

Your Data Stays Yours

All your data belongs exclusively to you. Every action, trigger, and permission remains under your direct, transparent control on your own device.

Seamless

Instant Integration

Once installed, Sky Vision automatically detects the AI Task Module. The AI Task card on your Home screen unlocks immediately with no complicated setup.

What the Module Unlocks

Extend Sky Vision with scheduled intelligence and smart triggers.

Smart Decision Triggers
