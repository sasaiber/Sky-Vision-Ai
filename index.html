Accessibility API Policy Update

Google updated its Developer Program Guidelines with a strict prohibition regarding Accessibility Service:

"Any use of the Accessibility API that enables an app to autonomously initiate, plan, and execute actions or decisions is strictly prohibited."
January 28, 2026

Google Play Enforcement & Architecture

Google began strictly enforcing this policy across Google Play. Apps distributed via the store that use Accessibility Service for autonomous AI routines face immediate rejection. To keep the primary Sky Vision app 100% compliant while keeping powerful automated routines accessible, background tasks are isolated in this dedicated companion app.
