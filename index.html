Why a separate app?

A quick explanation of why this module is installed as a companion APK.

Google Play Policy

Google Play does not allow store apps to run automated background accessibility actions. The main app adheres 100% to this policy.

Full Functionality

Instead of removing background routines, they are placed in this companion app so you can still use them without store limitations.

Your Data Stays Yours

All data stays on your device. Every task and permission runs under your direct control.

Automatic Setup

Once installed, Sky Vision detects the module automatically — no configuration needed.

What it does

Features unlocked with the companion module.

Scheduled Tasks

Set recurring or one-time AI queries (news summaries, price checks, reminders) in the background.

PriorityFlow Actions

Trigger automated device actions and responses when specific notifications arrive.
