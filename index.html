Google Play Policy Timeline

October 30, 2025

Policy Announcement

Google updated its Developer Program Guidelines with a strict prohibition regarding Accessibility Service:

"Any use of the Accessibility API that enables an app to autonomously initiate, plan, and execute actions or decisions is strictly prohibited."
January 28, 2026

Enforcement Begins

Google began strictly enforcing the policy across Google Play. Store-distributed apps using Accessibility Service for automated background AI actions face automatic rejection during review.

Architecture & Privacy

Full Capabilities Preserved

Your Data Stays Yours
